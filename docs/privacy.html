Privacy Policy

OWNLY NETWORK LLC ("we") operates the China News Feed data products: the Apify actor, the RapidAPI listing, the x402 pay-per-call API and this sample site. Last updated: October 28, 2026.

What the feed contains

The feed is built only from public, logged-out sources: news outlets' public pages and feeds, and public platform hot lists (for example Weibo, Baidu, Zhihu). We do not log in to any platform, do not collect private messages or accounts, and do not build profiles of individuals. Rows contain headlines, links, excerpts, ranks and timestamps as published.

What we collect from buyers

We do not collect personal data from feed buyers. We do not use cookies, analytics or trackers on this site or in the API responses. Our servers may keep short-lived technical logs (such as request time, path and status code) for security and debugging; we do not use them to identify buyers.

Payments and accounts are handled by third parties, who process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Apify — accounts, billing and usage for the Apify actor (apify.com/privacy-policy).
  • RapidAPI — accounts, subscriptions and billing for the RapidAPI listing (rapidapi.com/privacy).
  • Coinbase — x402 payment verification and settlement in USDC on the Base network (coinbase.com/legal/privacy). On-chain payments are public by nature; we see only the paying wallet address and amount.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub host the API and this site and may process standard connection data such as IP addresses.

We receive from these providers only what is needed to deliver and account for the service (for example a subscription plan or payment confirmation). We do not sell or share buyer data.
